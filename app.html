Briefing
Mission Briefing

Two captains, one device. Name your fleets, deploy five word-ships each, then hunt each other down letter by letter. The device is passed between turns — a privacy screen stands between you and your opponent's grid.

Both captains must use the same method.

Order of Battle

Fire on a coordinate. Miss and your turn ends. Hit and you may call an Alpha Strike — guess the letter hiding there. Wrong letter and the defender must announce how many times it appears across their whole fleet.

Victory comes one way: Demand Surrender and name every word-ship and its exact berth. Get it all right and you win outright. Get anything wrong and you lose on the spot.
