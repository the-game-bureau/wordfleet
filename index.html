The Word Fleet Game Notebook

Sink or Spell.

Open the notebook, grab a pencil, and challenge a friend to a duel of hidden words. Each battle sheet gives you one grid to hide your word-ships and another to track your shots, guesses, and clues.

  • 2 captains
  • 15–30 minutes
  • Ages 10+

Price to be announced

How it works

  1. Plot your fleet

    Each captain tears a Battle Tracker out of the notebook and keeps the printed words hidden from their opponent. Place your five words of 5, 4, 3, 3, and 2 letters on your Defense Grid, across or down, one letter per square.

  2. Bluff for the first turn

    Use the five launch codes on your sheet to decide who goes first. Call out a real or made-up code. The other captain can concede, challenge your code, or counter with an equal or higher number.

  3. Guess a word-ship to earn a shot

    Start your attack by guessing one whole enemy word-ship. A correct guess lets you call a coordinate and take a shot. A wrong guess ends your turn. If your shot hits, take one guess at the letter in that square.

  4. Demand surrender

    When you think you’ve found the entire fleet, name all five words and their exact locations. Get them all right and you win. One wrong word or location costs you the game.
