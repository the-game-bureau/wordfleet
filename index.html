The Word Fleet Game Notebook

Sink or Spell.

Hide your word-ships on a grid, then hunt your opponent’s fleet one coordinate and one letter at a time. All you need are two Battle Trackers and something to write with.

  • 2 captains
  • 15–30 minutes
  • Ages 10+
Print game sheets Read the rules Play Mobile Mode

How to play

  1. Hide your fleet

    Place five word-ships across your defense grid. Keep their locations and letters secret.

  2. Call your shot

    Pick a coordinate on your opponent’s grid. If you hit a ship, guess the letter in that square.

  3. Sink their ships

    Use the letters you uncover to work out the hidden words and sink the opposing fleet.
