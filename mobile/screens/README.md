# Mobile Mode screens

Screens are named `NNN-MOBILE-NAME` in the order players see them. A parked screen has no number.

Reference names for the screens of Mobile Mode (`mobile/index.html`), so a screen can be
referred to by name when asking for changes. Each has a reference snapshot in this folder.

| Name | In the code | Snapshot | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **MOBILE-HOME** *(parked)* | `<section id="scrMobileHome" data-screen="MOBILE-HOME">`, `SCREENS.home` | `mobile-home.png` | **Parked, not shown to players.** Kept for later use. Was the first screen: WORD FLEET title, Mobile Mode tag, blurb, New Battle and Rules of Engagement buttons. The app now opens on 001-MOBILE-PREPARE. |
| **001-MOBILE-PREPARE** | `<section id="scrSetup" data-screen="001-MOBILE-PREPARE">`, `SCREENS.setup` | `001-mobile-prepare.png` | **First screen players see.** Card title "Welcome Captain!". "Choose your language", then "Roll the die or type your own fleet name" (with dice). "Choose your color scheme": five two-color swatches (Navy & Green, Crimson & Gold, Teal & Coral, Midnight & Silver, Ocean & Sand), saved as `S.colors`; not applied anywhere yet. Changing language rolls a new fleet name in that language (`dictionaries/fleet-names.json`). Button "Next: Choose Your Opponent", filled 25% green as a progress bar. "← Back" goes to the Word Fleet home page (`../`, the site's `index.html`). Screens 001-004 share one look: a "PREPARE FOR BATTLE" page header above the card (`.page-head`), the step's card title, plain-language section headers (`.label--say`), and large, easy-to-read text (`.setup-big`). |
| **002-MOBILE-OPPONENT** | `<section id="scrOpponent" data-screen="002-MOBILE-OPPONENT">`, `SCREENS.opponent` | `002-mobile-opponent.png` | Card title "Choose Your Opponent", section "Pick the AI Captain you'll face": the three AI Captains (Captain Rubber Duck, Captain Steady, Captain Lexicon), each with a short character sketch. Button "Next: Build Your Word-Ships", filled 50% green. |
| **003-MOBILE-WORD-SHIPS** | `<section id="scrWords" data-screen="003-MOBILE-WORD-SHIPS">`, `SCREENS.words` | `003-mobile-word-ships.png` | Card title "Build Your Word-Ships". Section "Offensive words": a "Possibly Offensive Words OK" on/off switch, "Applies to both fleets." Section "Your five word-ships" (Refresh All at the right end of its header): "Tap any word to type your own." The five word-ships arrive drawn for your opponent and are editable, each with a ↻ redraw. A typed word is checked against the dictionary when its box loses focus, and every word is checked again on "Next: Deploy Your Fleet" (filled 75% green). |
| **004-MOBILE-DEPLOY** | `<section id="scrDeploy" data-screen="004-MOBILE-DEPLOY">`, `SCREENS.deploy` | `004-mobile-deploy.png` | Step 4 of 4 of Prepare for Battle, same look as 001-003. Card title "Deploy Your Fleet", then the Defense Grid dressed like the in-game one (navy "DEFENSE GRID" banner with your fleet name under it, navy top edge). Opens with the five word-ships already deployed at random, in green; they never leave the grid. Section "Keep this layout or rearrange it" with a Shuffle button (new random layout); drag a ship to move it, double-tap its first letter to turn it. "LET THE BATTLE BEGIN!" (progress bar full), Back. |
