# Mobile Mode screens

Screens are named `NNN-MOBILE-NAME` in the order players see them. Wording: the other side is the **AI Captain**; its fleet and word-ships are the **enemy** fleet and **enemy** word-ships ("opponent" is not used). A parked screen, or one reachable from anywhere (menu, rules), has no number.

Reference names for the screens of Mobile Mode (`mobile/index.html`), so a screen can be
referred to by name when asking for changes. Each has a reference snapshot in this folder.

| **005-MOBILE-ATTACK** | `<div id="tabAttack" data-screen="005-MOBILE-ATTACK">` in `#scrBattle`; coach bar `#coach` | `005-mobile-attack.png`, `005-mobile-bonus-turn.png` | Battle, Attack Grid tab: the Attack Grid under a banner in your fleet colors ("ATTACK GRID / Hunting the Enemy"), with the **Letters Manifest** strip under the grid: every letter not yet called, each dropping out once called: A to Z with the vowels as round chips, and a note at the right of the title: "VOWELS: −1 TURN". Tap a square to aim (the outline moves to the manifest, which lights up and its letters become buttons), then tap a letter to call it; tap another square to re-aim or the same square to cancel. There is no letter-picker popup. Called letters show on the grid. Open water (you aimed there and nothing is there) is pale sea blue with "≈" waves, on both grids. Your Defense Grid marks the AI Captain's squares the same way: an outline plus the letters it has ruled out. The **coach bar** above the tabs always says what to do next, with a step marker and at most two buttons: ① "Your turn. Tap a square to aim." (with everything since your last turn folded into one line above it, e.g. "AI: E at A9 (0) · you lost a turn (vowel)") → ② "Call a letter for D4" → ③ '"T tally 2." Revealed at D6, C6' with END TURN; ★ gold for a bonus turn; "AI Captain is aiming…" on its turn. The grid tells the story: new letters flip in one by one, the aimed square keeps a marker (gold ✓ when the letter was there), the called letter drops out of the Letters Manifest. Bonus turn also plays the bell-and-fanfare and flashes "★ BONUS TURN!". Longer hints for a captain's first two calls, or with Hints on in the ☰ menu. There is no Solve a Word button: revealed letters sit on white, and when every letter of a word-ship is revealed its squares all turn green, its ship circle turns green, and the coach bar says "LABEL is sunk!". Demand Surrender (009, ☰ menu only) is the only popup. The AI's turn no longer jumps to the Defense tab; its tab gets a red dot. |
